What exactly does Career Mastery Hub do?+
CMH is a career and global education advisory company. We guide students and professionals through Career Guidance & Psychometric Assessment, Foreign Admission Services, Equivalence & Recognition Assessment, Study Abroad Management, Crisis Recovery & Emergency Transfer, and talent pathways. Every service connects to one goal: a career outcome that actually works.
Is CMH just a study abroad agency?+
No. Study abroad is one part of CMH's work. Many clients come for career assessment, recognition review, test preparation, crisis recovery, or employment-readiness support. CMH was built to help people make safer education and career decisions, not only secure admissions.
Unanisaidiaje kabla sijafanya maamuzi?+

Where is CMH located?+
CMH is at 1040 Haile Sellasie Road, Masaki, Dar es Salaam. Reachable by phone, WhatsApp, or email. Open Mon-Fri 8am-6pm and Saturday 9am-2pm.
How is CMH different from other agencies?+
CMH takes a recognition-first approach by checking TQF alignment, TCU recognition considerations, and professional licensing requirements before recommending a foreign university route. Most admission conversations focus on getting in. CMH also asks whether the degree can support the career after return.
